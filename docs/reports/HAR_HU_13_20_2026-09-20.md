## Accounting boundaries

Confirmed provider token usage and estimated USD are separate. Prices require source, effective date and expiry; absent or expired prices leave cost unknown and prevent calls under a monetary cap. Unknown failed/interrupted calls also prevent further capped calls. A resumed run uses its durable original policy; importing a snapshot without its ledger cannot reset its cap. Older runs without ledger history are identified as incomplete history.

Limits apply per logical run, including its child calls and resumptions. A new chat turn starts a new run. Preflight input reservation is a heuristic and estimated cost is not an invoice or a guaranteed billing ceiling. Monetary limits require a supported output cap; unsupported Qwen caps fail closed. Existing legacy token/cost policies remain distinct and conflicting monetary policies are rejected.
