## Preparatory implementation

- `scripts/acceptance/pilot-protocol.ts` reuses HU44 fixtures/revision. Strict plan with artifact/configuration/model identity, tasks, budgets, approval, retries, repetitions and predeclared rotating order. It distinguishes same-model from product comparison; the first mode rejects provider/route/settings differences.
- Complete journal validator and summary: preserves failures/retries/blockers, planned denominators, missing rows and pending attempts. Rejects mixed identities, altered order, duplicates, retries after success/blocking and successes exceeding reported limits.
- Missing usage/cost/intervention remain null. Automatic approval and scripted correction are recorded separately from human intervention; costs require a pricing reference and are not invoices. Incomplete budget evidence remains explicit.
- `scripts/competitive-pilot.ts` generates a plan lock and JSON summary offline, reading bounded regular files and creating outputs without overwriting. It does not execute third-party tools or call models.
- Local methodology in `docs/COMPETITIVE_PILOT.md`. Hashes bind supplied observations; they neither authenticate the evaluator nor prove referenced certification. A valid plan does not authorize a campaign or establish results.
